# Worthy Numbers, change log

## 1.0 · 22 October 2026
First release. Eleven A5 pages: cover, contents, six numbers about your food (the label, Nutri-Score, protein claims, protein quality, GI and GL, protein density), one bowl with every number, about the author, and the back cover with the way into Chef Mealan.
Before release: editor, product and reader reviews, a blind proofread that recalculated every number and checked every source, and a final typesetting pass (numbers kept with their units, page 10 aligned to the pixel).
Published at chefmealan.com/worthy-numbers with the Chef Mealan release 0.2.72.
