Glycaemic index · glycaemic load

GL is GI with your portion in it.

GI ranks carbohydrate foods from 0 to 100 by how much they raise blood glucose over two hours, against pure glucose at 100. Labs test it on a fixed amount of carbohydrate, not on your portion.

GL brings your portion in. A GL of 10 or less counts as low, 11 to 19 as medium, and 20 or more as high. Packs rarely print GI: look it up in the University of Sydney GI database at glycemicindex.com.

GL = GI × grams of carbohydrate ÷ 100
