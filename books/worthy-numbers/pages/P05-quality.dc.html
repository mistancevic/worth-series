Protein quality · PDCAAS and DIAAS

20 g of protein is not always the same 20 g.

Protein consists of amino acids, and some of them only food can give you. A quality score checks two things: whether the protein brings enough of each, and how much of it your body takes up.

PDCAAS is the older score. It caps every protein at 1.0, so the best ones look the same. DIAAS, which the FAO recommended in 2013, measures each amino acid at the end of the small intestine, where your body takes it up. It has no cap.
