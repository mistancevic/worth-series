Nutri-Score

The letter grades the product, not your meal.

Nutri-Score gives a product one letter from A to E, calculated per 100 g or 100 ml. France started it in 2017. Belgium, Germany, Luxembourg, the Netherlands, Spain and Switzerland back it too. It is voluntary, so many packs carry no letter. An updated formula has applied since 2024 in most of these countries.

The letter sums up one product. Energy, sugars, saturates and salt add points. Protein, fibre, and fruit, vegetables and legumes take points off. The total becomes the letter. It leaves out how much you eat, what you eat it with and what your day needs.
