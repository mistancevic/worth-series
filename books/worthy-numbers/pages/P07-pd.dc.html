PD · protein density

PD: one number for protein against energy.

PD is grams of protein per 100 kcal. No pack prints it. You calculate it from two lines of the label: protein ÷ kcal × 100. Granola: 9 g ÷ 450 kcal × 100 = PD 2.

PD is the protein claim from page 5 in another unit. Protein gives 4 kcal per gram, so Source of protein (12%) is PD 3, and High protein (20%) is PD 5. Those lines are the same for everyone.

Your own target comes from your goal, your body and your training. Two examples: a man of 80 kg who trains twice a week and wants to keep his weight needs about 2,350 kcal and 150 g of protein. A woman of 66 kg who trains three times a week and wants to build muscle needs about 1,950 kcal and 125 g. Different bodies, goals and amounts, and both land on PD 6.4. Chef Mealan calculates your own target with you and shows every step of the sum.
