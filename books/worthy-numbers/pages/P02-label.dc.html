The label

The label counts per 100 g, not per bowl.

Almost every pack in the EU lists the same values per 100 g or 100 ml: energy in kJ and kcal, fat and saturates, carbohydrate and sugars, protein and salt. A value per portion is optional, and the portion is the maker’s guess.

Use per 100 g to compare two packs on the shelf. Your bowl is a different number: what you pour, plus what you pour over it.
