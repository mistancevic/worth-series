Source of protein · High protein

“High protein” is a share of the energy, not an amount.

In the EU, a pack may say Source of protein when at least 12% of its energy comes from protein, and High protein when at least 20% does.

A share says nothing about how many grams of protein your portion gives you. The PD formula gives this share as one number for your whole meal. You find it on page 8.
